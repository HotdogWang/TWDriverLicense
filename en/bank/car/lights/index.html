Car written test: Lights and signals

This category tests when to use high and low beams and turn signals. Use low beams when driving in town at night, when passing oncoming traffic, or when within 100 metres of the vehicle ahead, to avoid dazzling others. Signal at least 30 metres before an intersection when turning, and keep the signal on until a lane change or turn is completed. Turn on the headlights in thick fog.

Highway Bureau category: Proper Use of Lights (Headlights, Fog Lights, Turn Signals); Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle); 28 questions in this category.
