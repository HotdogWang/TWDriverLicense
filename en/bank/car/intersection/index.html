Car written test: Intersection Safety (Signalized / Unsignalized Intersections, Yielding to Pedestrians)

This category tests the order of giving way at intersections and the meaning of signals. Slow down when approaching a pedestrian crossing, and stop to let pedestrians go first when they are crossing; at an intersection with no marked crossing, you must also stop to let crossing pedestrians go first, whether or not there is a traffic officer or signal. At an uncontrolled intersection, the vehicle on the branch road gives way to the vehicle on the main road. Where there is no distinction between main and branch roads, the number of lanes entering the intersection is equal and both vehicles are going straight, the vehicle on the left gives way to the vehicle on the right. A flashing red light means stop and then proceed; a flashing yellow light means slow down, take care and proceed with caution.
