Car written test: Vehicle Inspection (Equipment, Lights)

This category tests pre-drive vehicle checks and dashboard warnings. To check the oil level, wait a moment after the engine is off so the oil can drain back before measuring. Check tyre pressure before driving, when the tyres are cold; heavier wear on the centre of the tread usually means the pressure is too high. If the temperature gauge goes past its upper limit while driving, pull over safely, switch off the engine and check the radiator only after the engine has cooled. A privately owned passenger car (except one running on LPG or CNG) needs no periodic inspection until it is 5 years past its factory date, at least 1 inspection a year from 5 to under 10 years, and at least 2 a year from 10 years on.
