Car written test: Sharing the road with large vehicles

This category tests the rear-wheel off-tracking and blind spots of large vehicles. The longer the wheelbase, the greater the difference between the paths of the inner and outer wheels when turning, and the more road the vehicle needs. When a large vehicle is about to turn right, do not stop at its front right or on the inside of the turn, or you may be caught in the off-tracking zone and blind spot and dragged under. The idea that seeing a large vehicle's driver means the driver can see you is wrong; when following a large vehicle, keep a safe distance and watch what the vehicle ahead is doing.

Highway Bureau category: Watching Out for Large Vehicles (Inner Wheel Difference, Blind Spots, No Side-by-Side Driving); Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle); 32 questions in this category.
