Car written test: Driving behaviour

This category tests following distance, speed limits and overtaking. In normal weather, the safe distance in metres between small vehicles on freeways and expressways is the speed in km/h divided by 2; at 90 km/h, for example, keep at least 45 metres. Where there is no speed limit sign or marking, the speed may not exceed 50 km/h; on roads with no lane lines, centre line or no-overtaking line, it may not exceed 30 km/h. To overtake a vehicle in the same lane, first sound the horn with 2 short beeps or flash the lights once, and overtake only after the vehicle ahead slows down and moves aside or signals you to pass. Overtaking is not allowed on the acceleration lanes, deceleration lanes and single-lane ramps of freeways and expressways.
