Car written test: Hazard Perception Videos

This is the hazard perception video category: you watch a video of car driving, and the question asks what you should do after watching it. For example, when an oncoming car keeps its turn signal on without slowing as it approaches an intersection, or a motorcycle approaches an uncontrolled intersection from the side without slowing down, you should slow down first and then confirm it is safe or get ready to stop. The website does not host the videos; each question links to the Highway Bureau's video sharing page, and in the app you can download the videos to watch offline.

Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle); 10 questions in this category. Source: the Highway Bureau's published question bank. Translations are for reference only. The Chinese version prevails. Chinese original (中文)

    An oncoming vehicle continues to display its turn signal and approaches the intersection without slowing down. What should you do?

    1. Slow down first and proceed only after confirming it is safe. Correct answer
    2. Honk or flash your lights to remind the vehicle to let you pass first.
    3. Vehicles going straight have priority; assume the other vehicle will yield to me.

    When starting on a green light, you notice a vehicle from the cross direction showing no sign of slowing down and rapidly approaching the intersection. What should you do?

    1. Delay starting and brake to a stop; proceed only after confirming the cross-traffic vehicle has stopped or passed. Correct answer
    2. Assume the other vehicle will stop at the intersection, and start at normal speed.
    3. Since the light is green for me, start immediately and honk to remind the other vehicle to stop.
