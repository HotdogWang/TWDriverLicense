Car written test: Accident prevention and handling

This category tests what to do after an accident and how to prevent a second one. If there is an accident or breakdown on a freeway and the vehicle cannot be moved out of the lane, besides turning on the hazard lights, place a breakdown warning sign at least 100 metres behind the vehicle. On a road where the speed is 40 km/h or less, if the vehicle breaks down and cannot be moved, place a warning sign 5 to 30 metres behind it on the road surface. When nobody is injured or killed and the vehicle can still be driven, first mark or photograph the positions of the vehicles and the point of impact, then move the vehicles to where they do not block traffic; when someone is injured or killed, keep the scene intact and call the police.

Highway Bureau category: Accident Prevention & Handling (Secondary Highway Accidents, First Aid); Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle); 85 questions in this category.
