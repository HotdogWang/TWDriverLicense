Car written test: Prohibited Misconduct (Drunk Driving, Mobile Phone Use, Dangerous Driving)

This category tests the rules and penalties on drink driving, hand-held phones and dangerous driving. A driver's breath alcohol concentration must stay below 0.15 mg per litre. Even if you feel sober after drinking, alcohol still affects your reaction and judgement, so take a taxi or have a designated driver drive you. For a first refusal of an alcohol test, the fine is NT$180,000, the vehicle is impounded on the spot, the licence is revoked, and you must attend a road safety course. While driving you may not use a phone by hand to dial, talk or use data; the fine is NT$3,000.
