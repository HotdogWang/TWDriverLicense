Taiwan driving licence written test questions 2026 (with answers)

The written test is multiple choice: 50 questions, 2 points each, 85 to pass. Questions follow the Highway Bureau's three frameworks: Correct Concepts and Ethics, Proactive Yielding Culture, Safe Driving Ability. Motorcycles also have situational questions; both vehicles have hazard perception video questions. Every question and its correct answer is listed here by vehicle and category.

Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle). Source: the Highway Bureau's published question bank; the actual test follows the Bureau's announcements. Translations are for reference only. The Chinese version prevails. Chinese original (中文)
