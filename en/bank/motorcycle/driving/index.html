Motorcycle written test: Driving behaviour

This category tests motorcycle speed limits, riding lanes and safe spacing. Where there is no speed limit sign or marking, the speed may not exceed 50 km/h; on roads with no lane lines, centre line or no-overtaking line, it may not exceed 30 km/h, and in the slow lane of a road with a fast and slow lane divider it may not exceed 40 km/h. Motorcycles other than large heavy motorcycles, on a two-way road with no signs or markings saying otherwise, must ride in the outermost 2 lanes when there are no separate fast and slow lanes; when fast and slow lanes are separated, they must ride in the outermost fast lane and the slow lane. Keep at least 0.5 metres from vehicles beside you, and at least 1 metre, about one car door's width, when passing cars parked at the roadside.

Highway Bureau category: Driving Precautions (Safe Following Distance, Watching Ahead); Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle); 172 questions in this category.
