Motorcycle written test: Vehicle Inspection (Equipment, Lights)

This category tests motorcycle condition checks and helmets. When you lightly pull the brake lever, there should be 1 to 2 cm of free play; on a scooter-type motorcycle, the right brake lever controls the front wheel and the left one controls the rear wheel. On dry roads or downhill sections, mainly use the front brake with the rear brake as support; on wet and slippery roads or uphill sections, mainly use the rear brake with the front brake as support. Replace a tyre when the tread has worn down level with the wear indicator, and a helmet should not be used again after it has taken a strong impact.
