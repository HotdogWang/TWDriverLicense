Motorcycle written test: Accident prevention and handling

This category tests what to do after an accident and first-aid basics. When nobody is injured or killed and the motorcycle can still be ridden, first mark or photograph the position of the vehicles, then move them to where they do not block traffic, and you should still notify the police. The first-aid order is to keep the airway open first, then stop bleeding, and finally immobilise fractures; if a neck injury is suspected, carefully steady the head and neck first, then open the airway with the jaw-thrust method. For adult CPR, the ratio of chest compressions to rescue breaths is 30 to 2.

Highway Bureau category: Accident Prevention & Handling; Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle); 62 questions in this category.
