Motorcycle written test: Hazard Perception Videos

This is the hazard perception video category: you watch a video of motorcycle riding, and the question asks what you should do after watching it. For example, when a roadside vehicle signals that it is about to cut into the lane, slow down, watch it and be ready to stop at any time; when you notice a pedestrian stepping into the lane in an alley, slow down, stop and let the pedestrian go first. The website does not host the videos; each question links to the Highway Bureau's video sharing page, and in the app you can download the videos to watch offline.

Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle); 126 questions in this category. Source: the Highway Bureau's published question bank. Translations are for reference only. The Chinese version prevails. Chinese original (中文)
