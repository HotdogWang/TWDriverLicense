Motorcycle written test: Sharing the road with large vehicles

This category tests safety awareness for motorcycles sharing the road with large vehicles. When a large vehicle ahead signals a right turn, slow down and stay behind it at a distance, to avoid entering the off-tracking zone on its right side; the longer the wheelbase, the greater the difference between the paths of the inner and outer wheels, and a passenger car also has off-tracking when it turns. Avoid riding alongside large vehicles and keep enough space from them; when you find a large vehicle behind you, you can move to the right and slow down, or give way appropriately so it can overtake.

Highway Bureau category: Watching Out for Large Vehicles (Inner Wheel Difference, Blind Spots, No Side-by-Side Driving); Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle); 13 questions in this category.     2. The longer the wheelbase, the greater the off-tracking

  6. No. 565中文

    Regarding the violation standards for running a red light, which of the following statements is correct?

    1. When the traffic signal facing the vehicle is not red, the vehicle crosses the stop line, but stops within the intersection area after the red light turns on
    2. When facing a circular red light, the rider pushes (or rides) the motorcycle to a road in another direction where the signal is not red and then continues riding Correct answer
    3. After the red light turns on, the vehicle's front wheels cross the stop line but do not obstruct cross traffic of pedestrians or vehicles.

    1. Speed up to maintain distance.

    3. Stop immediately.

    2. Avoid riding side by side with large vehicles and keep a sufficient safe distance. Correct answer

  13. No. 572中文

    When riding and noticing a large vehicle ahead signaling a right turn, how should the motorcyclist respond?

    1. Accelerate and overtake on the right side while the large vehicle is slowing down
