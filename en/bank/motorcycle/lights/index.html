Motorcycle written test: Lights and signals

This category tests when to use a motorcycle's headlight, fog light and turn signals. Use the low beam when passing oncoming traffic at night, following another vehicle, or riding in a well-lit town; besides at night, also turn on the headlight in tunnels, culverts and reversible lanes, or when thick fog or heavy rain makes it hard to see, and in thick fog in the daytime turn on the headlight and the fog light. Before changing lanes, signal early, check the rear-view mirror and turn your head to look; entering the waiting area from the outermost lane needs no turn signal.

Highway Bureau category: Proper Use of Lights (Headlights, Fog Lights, Turn Signals); Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle); 28 questions in this category.
