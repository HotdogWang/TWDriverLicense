Motorcycle written test: Prohibited Misconduct (Drunk Driving, Mobile Phone Use, Dangerous Driving)

This category tests drink driving, phone use and dangerous driving. Breath alcohol concentration must stay below 0.15 mg per litre; a motorcycle rider caught drink driving for the second time within 10 years is fined NT$90,000 and has the licence revoked for 3 years. For a first refusal of an alcohol test, the fine is NT$180,000 and the licence is revoked on the spot. While riding you may not use a phone by hand; pull over to the roadside before using it. Anyone who causes an accident by driving dangerously, such as weaving or riding on the rear wheel only, is fined and has the licence revoked.
