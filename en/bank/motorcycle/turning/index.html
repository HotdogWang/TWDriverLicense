Motorcycle written test: Turns and U-turns

This category tests two-stage left turns, waiting areas and the timing of turn signals. For a turn, first follow the signs or markings; at an intersection with a sign for a two-stage left turn for motorcycles and slow vehicles, you must make a two-stage left turn regardless of traffic volume or time of day; where there are no turning instructions and the inner lane has a sign or marking banning motorcycles, make a two-stage left turn. Crossing the stop line into the waiting area on a red light, or riding out of the waiting area before the signal for your direction allows you to go straight, counts as running a red light. At an intersection where a two-stage left turn is not required, signal at least 30 metres before the intersection and move into the inner lane or left-turn lane.

Highway Bureau category: Turning (Left / Right Turns, U-turns); Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle); 34 questions in this category.
