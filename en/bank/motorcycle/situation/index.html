Motorcycle written test: Situational Questions

Motorcycle situational questions are about judging from a picture: each question includes an illustration of a traffic situation, and from the vehicle positions, lanes and markings shown you decide how to ride safely. Common situations include not entering the off-tracking zone shown in the picture when a large vehicle turns right, stopping to let a pedestrian who is crossing go first when turning right, and the branch-road vehicle giving way to the main-road vehicle at an uncontrolled intersection.

Question bank version: Highway Bureau 115.06 (car) / 115.02 (motorcycle); 120 questions in this category. Source: the Highway Bureau's published question bank. Translations are for reference only. The Chinese version prevails. Chinese original (中文)
