Motorcycle written test: Cargo Loading

This category tests the size and weight limits for goods carried on a motorcycle. The load may not be higher than the rider's shoulders or wider than 10 cm beyond the outer edge of the handlebars, may not extend more than half a metre behind the rear axle, and may not extend forward of the rear of the seat. The weight limit is 80 kg for a heavy motorcycle and 50 kg for an ordinary light motorcycle; once a passenger is carried, no other goods may be carried, except small items that do not affect the safety of the rider and passenger.
