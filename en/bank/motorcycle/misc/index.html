Motorcycle written test: Signs and general knowledge

This category is very broad. It covers level crossings, compulsory insurance, eco-driving, special weather and driver ethics, plus questions on recognising signs. After you see a railway level-crossing sign or marking, slow down to 15 km/h or less. At a crossing with a barrier, even if the barrier is not down, you must still look and listen and cross only when no train is coming from either direction; at a crossing with no attendant, barrier, bell or flashing signal, stop, look and listen 3 to 6 metres from the track. In the rain, avoid braking hard or turning sharply on road markings, manhole covers or steel plates; slow down and keep as straight a line as possible while riding over them.
